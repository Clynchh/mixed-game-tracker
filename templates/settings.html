This is a plain folder path — MixTrack only reads .txt files inside it on an interval. It never touches the PokerStars client or process, so it's not a HUD and doesn't interact with the client while you play. Find yours in PokerStars: Settings → Hand History → Hand History Location.
Required for accurate results in stud/razz games. Hold'em and Omaha hands only ever show your own hole cards, so guessing "hero" by position works fine — but stud/razz hands show every player's up-cards each street, so without your exact username the tracker can misidentify another player at the table as you and get your net result completely wrong.
